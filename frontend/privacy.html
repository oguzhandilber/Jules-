Privacy Policy for Clarity

Last updated: June 15, 2025

Welcome to Clarity! We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your video files and data when you use our service.

Information We Collect

When you upload a video file for caption removal, we temporarily store this file on our servers to perform the requested processing.

How We Use Your Files

Your uploaded video files are used solely for the purpose of removing captions as requested by you. We do not view, share, or use your video content for any other purpose.

File Storage and Deletion

We understand the importance of your data's security and transience. Our file handling policy is as follows:

  • Original Uploaded Files: The original video file you upload is deleted from our active servers by the processing worker after it has attempted caption removal (whether successful or not).
  • Processed Files (Caption-Removed Videos): The processed video file (with captions removed) is made available for you to download.
    • This processed file will be automatically and permanently deleted from our servers 24 hours after the original upload time. (This is a planned feature for Milestone 3; currently, files are not automatically deleted based on time).
    • Alternatively, if you download the processed file, it will be automatically and permanently deleted 1 hour after your first download. (This is a planned feature for Milestone 3).
  • For the current MVP, processed files are not automatically deleted based on time or download count. This will be implemented in a future milestone.

This policy is designed to give you adequate time to download your processed file while ensuring your data is not stored on our servers indefinitely once fully implemented.

Data Security

We implement reasonable measures to protect the security of your files during the upload, processing, and storage period. All connections for uploading and downloading files should be made over HTTPS (this will be configured during deployment).

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
